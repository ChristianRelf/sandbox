Settings are divided by responsibility so product preferences do not become workflow permissions.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="General" icon="sliders-horizontal" href="/settings/general">Start screen, date/time display, and unsaved-navigation confirmation.</Card>
  <Card title="Appearance" icon="palette" href="/settings/appearance">Theme, dark surface, accent, density, and sidebar width.</Card>
  <Card title="Accessibility" icon="accessibility" href="/settings/accessibility">Reduced motion, contrast, and accessible-editor default.</Card>
  <Card title="Node editor" icon="workflow" href="/settings/node-editor">Minimap, grid, hints, descriptions, and deletion confirmation.</Card>
  <Card title="Connections" icon="plug-zap" href="/settings/connections">AI providers and models, Gmail PKCE, and Discord/Slack webhook credentials.</Card>
  <Card title="Browser" icon="globe" href="/settings/browser">Engine health and isolated profile management.</Card>
  <Card title="Beta & updates" icon="refresh-cw" href="/settings/beta-and-updates">Installed version, release check, and channel.</Card>
</CardGroup>

Preferences apply to the current desktop app. They do not silently alter a saved workflow's graph, permissions, or published revision.

<Info>
  Desktop settings are separate from the signed-in account area. Use [Security and account settings](/account/security-and-settings) for sessions, API keys, data export, sign-out, and account deletion.
</Info>
