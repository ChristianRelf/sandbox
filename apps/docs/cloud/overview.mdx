Signing in adds organisations, workspaces, encrypted workflow sync, approval/publication, shared resources, and remote runners. Personal local execution remains available without an account.

## Local, synced, and published are different

| State | Meaning | Who can execute it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local draft | Stored on this device and editable without an account | The desktop local runner, subject to local permissions |
| Synced revision | An encrypted exact revision is associated with a cloud owner | Authorized workspace users and services according to role and policy |
| Approved revision | Reviewers approved one content identity | It is eligible for publication; later edits are not implicitly approved |
| Published revision | The approved identity is active for its environment and runner policy | Compatible authorized runners |

Sync is not publication, and publication is not a mutable pointer to whatever is currently on the canvas. This separation keeps review evidence attached to the bytes that actually run.

## Ownership stays explicit

Every shared resource carries an `ownerType` and `ownerId`. A personal workflow is not inferred to belong to a workspace after sign-in. Import or sync it deliberately when you want workspace governance.

Connections, browser profiles, paths, and local protected values do not automatically become shared merely because a workflow revision is synced. Review missing environment resources before assigning a remote runner.

## What the Cloud view handles

- account and workspace selection;
- local and cloud revision status;
- upload/download of encrypted workflow revisions;
- conflict resolution without silent graph merging;
- approval requests and votes;
- publication of an approved exact revision; and
- workspace/runner context needed for remote work.

## Local continuity

During a control-plane outage, local edits and local executions continue. Sync, marketplace, remote command, and cloud status report unavailable or waiting instead of blocking a personal local workflow.

## First workspace workflow

1. Choose the intended organisation, workspace, and environment.
2. Sync a reviewed local revision and resolve any conflict explicitly.
3. Replace local-only resources with environment-owned connections, variables, profiles, and runner capabilities.
4. Request approval for the exact revision.
5. Publish only after the approval threshold is met.
6. Run a bounded test and inspect workspace audit history and execution evidence.
