# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

sndbox is committed to a welcoming, safe, and productive community. Everyone
participating in project spaces is expected to act with respect regardless of
experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and requests to stop an interaction.
- Credit other people's work and disclose conflicts of interest.
- Help keep discussions focused and accessible to newcomers.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, insults, stalking, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, sustained
disruption, or publishing another person's private information without consent.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, project
Discord spaces, support interactions, events, and public situations where a
person is representing sndbox.

## Reporting and enforcement

For immediate threats, contact local emergency services. Report GitHub content
that violates GitHub's terms using GitHub's reporting tools. Other project
conduct concerns can be submitted privately through
<https://app.sndbox.app/support>. Do not open a public issue about a conduct
incident. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Include links, dates, context, and any relevant screenshots, while minimizing
the personal data you share. Reports will be handled as confidentially as is
practical and reviewed for conflicts of interest. A maintainer named in a report
must not adjudicate that report; an independent moderator or platform operator
will be asked to review it.

Proportionate responses may include a private warning, removal of content,
temporary restrictions, removal from project spaces, or a permanent ban.
Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.

Maintainers may remove contributions or comments that violate this policy and
will explain an enforcement decision to the affected person when safe and
practical.
